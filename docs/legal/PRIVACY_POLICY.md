## What we collect

We collect only what the service needs to work. There is no advertising, no
tracking, and no third-party analytics.

### You give us

| Data | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Email address | To identify your account, sign you in, and send password resets |
| Name | Shown back to you in the app |
| Password | Stored only as a bcrypt hash — we never see or store the password itself |
| Your goal, and why it matters | To generate and personalise your 30-day plan |
| Your Day 30 success definition | Shown back to you during and at the end of the challenge |
| Available time, obstacles, preferred time, difficulty | To shape a plan that fits your life |
| Daily reflections and notes | Kept for you, and to spot patterns in your own record |
| Weekly review answers | To adjust the coming week |
| Optional daily priorities | Your own list |

### Created as you use the app

| Data | Why |
| --- | --- |

| Minimum Days used | Counted as showing up |

### Stored on your device only

Your theme choice and reminder settings are stored on your device and never sent
to us. Your session token is held in the iOS Keychain.

### We do not collect

Location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone data, health or fitness
measurements, financial account details, advertising identifiers, or any
cross-app or cross-site activity.

We do not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission because we do not track.

## About your reflections

Your reflections, goals and reasons are the most personal thing SelfMastery
holds. We treat them accordingly:

- They are shown back only to you.
- They are never sold, shared or published.
- They are not used to train machine-learning models.
- We do not log their contents in our application logs.
- They are permanently deleted when you delete your account.

## Security

- All traffic uses HTTPS.
- Passwords are hashed with bcrypt and never stored in plain text.
- Session tokens are stored hashed on our servers, and in the Keychain on your
  device. A database leak does not hand over live sessions.
- Signing out revokes that device's token; changing your password revokes every
  session.
- Every request is checked against the account that owns the data.

No system is perfectly secure, and we do not claim otherwise.

## Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time, from inside the app:

**Profile → Delete account**

You will be shown what is removed and asked to confirm with your password.
Deletion removes your account and all associated data — challenges, daily
actions, completion history, reflections, weekly reviews and sessions.

It is permanent, immediate, and cannot be undone. There is no deactivation
alternative, and you do not need to contact us to do it.
