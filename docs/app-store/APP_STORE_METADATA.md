## Description

```
SelfMastery gives you one meaningful goal and thirty days to move toward it.

Not another habit tracker asking what you want to track. SelfMastery asks what
you want to change — then works out what you should do today.

CHOOSE ONE GOAL
Study more consistently. Become more active. Find a better job. Take control of
your money. Spend real time with your family. Read again. Finish the thing you
keep restarting. Or describe something in your own words.

GET A PLAN THAT FITS YOUR ACTUAL LIFE
Answer a few questions — how much time you genuinely have, what usually gets in
your way, when you're most likely to act — and SelfMastery builds thirty days of
small, specific actions. Week one is deliberately easy. The plan grows only once
showing up has stopped being a decision.

KNOW WHAT TO DO TODAY
Open the app and see today's actions. Nothing else competes for attention. Tick
them off as you go.

MINIMUM DAY
Some days fall apart. Instead of skipping, switch to a Minimum Day: the same
commitment at its smallest size. A thirty-minute walk becomes five minutes.
Reduce the requirement, not the commitment.

REFLECT IN SECONDS
One tap to say how the day felt. A note if you want one. Nothing longer.

ADJUST EVERY WEEK
Every seven days you look back for two minutes. If the week was hard, next week
gets lighter — SelfMastery never responds to a difficult week by asking for
more. Days you've already completed are never rewritten, and every change to
your plan comes with the reason for it.

SEE WHAT YOU ACTUALLY DID
Your calendar, your consistency, and observations drawn from your own record —
never invented. On Day 30, what changed, in your words and in real numbers.

BUILT TO BE CALM
No streak warnings. No guilt. No badges or confetti. A missed day isn't a
failure, and the app never tells you it is.

SelfMastery is for anyone with something they've been meaning to change.
```

### Claims to keep out

No guaranteed outcomes, no medical, therapeutic or financial claims, no
competitor names. Health, fitness, sleep and money goals stay behavioural in the
app, and the description must not promise more than that.

## Keywords (100 characters, comma-separated, no spaces)

```
goals,habits,discipline,focus,routine,productivity,self improvement,challenge,planning,motivation
```

That is 99 characters. Notes:

- The app name is already indexed; do not repeat "selfmastery".
- Singular and plural are matched automatically — do not spend characters on both.
- No competitor brand names. It breaks the guidelines and gets metadata rejected.

## Categories

**Primary: Productivity.** The product is about deciding what to do today and
doing it. Its closest neighbours — planners, task and focus apps — live in
Productivity, and that is where someone with this problem looks.

**Secondary: Lifestyle.** The goals are personal rather than professional:
health, family, money, reading. Health & Fitness was considered and rejected —
fitness is one of eighteen categories, and being listed there would misrepresent
a general-purpose product and invite health-app scrutiny that does not apply.
