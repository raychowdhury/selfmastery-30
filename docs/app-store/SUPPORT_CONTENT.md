## SelfMastery Support

### What is SelfMastery?

SelfMastery gives you one goal and thirty days to move toward it. You tell it
what you want to change, how much time you realistically have and what usually
gets in your way. It turns that into a few small actions each day, and adjusts as
you go.

It is not a habit tracker. You do not tell it what to track — you tell it what
you want to change, and it works out what today should look like.

### How does the 30-day challenge work?

Setup takes about two minutes. After that, opening the app shows today's
actions — usually two to four, sized to the time you said you had.

The plan grows deliberately slowly:

- **Days 1–7 — Consistency.** Small on purpose. The point is showing up.
- **Days 8–14 — Build.** Structure, now the habit exists.
- **Days 15–21 — Depth.** Real progress rather than just turning up.
- **Days 22–30 — Finish.** Toward whatever you said Day 30 should look like.

Every seven days you spend two minutes reviewing. If the week was hard, the next
one gets lighter.

### What is a Minimum Day?

Some days fall apart. Instead of skipping, tap **Use Minimum Day** and the plan
shrinks to its smallest meaningful version — a thirty-minute walk becomes five
minutes, sixty minutes of study becomes ten.

It still counts toward your consistency. You can switch back at any time, and
nothing you have already completed is lost.

Reduce the requirement, not the commitment.

### I missed a day. Have I ruined it?

No. A missed day is a gap in the record, not a failure, and nothing resets. The
app will not warn you about a streak or tell you that you have broken anything.

### How do I reset my password?

On the sign-in screen, tap **Forgotten your password?** and enter your email.
If that address has an account, we send a link that expires in 30 minutes.

The link opens a page where you choose a new password. For safety, changing it
signs you out on every device.

If the email does not arrive, check spam, and confirm you are using the address
you signed up with. For privacy we do not confirm whether an address is
registered, so the confirmation screen looks the same either way.

### My progress is not syncing

Everything you do is saved to your account, so it follows you across devices.

If the app shows *"You're offline. Your progress will sync when you're
connected."*, it could not reach our servers. Check your connection, then pull
down on the Today screen to refresh. If it persists for more than a few minutes,
contact us — it may be on our side.

### How do I change my reminders?

**Profile → Reminders.** You can turn each one on or off:

- Morning — today's plan is ready
- Your usual time — based on when you said you would act
- Evening — close out the day
- Weekly review

We never ask for notification permission when you first open the app, and
declining changes nothing else.

### How do I delete my account?

**Profile → Delete account**, at the bottom.

You will be shown exactly what is removed and asked for your password. Deletion
is permanent and immediate: your account, every challenge, all your daily
actions, completion history, reflections and weekly reviews. It cannot be
undone, and we cannot recover it afterwards.

There is no "deactivate" option. If you delete, it is gone.

### Can I run more than one challenge at once?

Not in this version, and that is intentional — the product's entire premise is
one meaningful goal at a time. Finishing or archiving a challenge lets you start
another, and completed challenges stay in **Profile → Previous challenges**.

### Is SelfMastery medical, therapeutic or financial advice?

No. It suggests general behavioural actions only — "walk for twenty minutes",
"record what you spent today". It does not diagnose, treat, prescribe, or advise
on money.

For anything affecting your health, mental health or finances, speak to a
qualified professional.
