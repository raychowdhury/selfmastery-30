## Why not a higher rating

**Medical/treatment information: None** deserves justification, because the app
serves health goals.

SelfMastery never gives medical, dietary, therapeutic or clinical guidance.
Health-adjacent goals produce general behavioural prompts — "Walk for 20
minutes", "Note what you ate today", "Wind down for 15 minutes before bed" — and
those categories carry an explicit safety note during onboarding stating the app
is not a medical or professional service. Nothing diagnoses, treats, measures, or
recommends a dosage, diet or exercise programme.

The same holds for money goals: recording and reviewing your own spending, with
a note that the app is not a financial adviser. No investment, tax or debt
guidance is produced.

## Minimum age

No age gate. The app has no restricted content and collects no data from
children beyond an email address and what the person writes themselves.

The Terms of Use set a minimum age of 13 (16 where local law requires it), and
the Privacy Policy states the service is not directed at children under 13. Keep
those two consistent if either changes.
