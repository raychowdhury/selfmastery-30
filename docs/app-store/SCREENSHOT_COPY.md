# Screenshot copy

Mature and restrained. The product's voice is a calm coach, not a cheerleader,
and the store listing is the first place people hear it.

## The six headlines

```
1.  One meaningful goal.
2.  Know exactly what matters today.
3.  Progress without perfection.
4.  See your consistency grow.
5.  Reflect. Adjust. Improve.
6.  See what changed in 30 days.
```

## Optional subheads

Only if the frame has room. The headline should work alone.

| # | Subhead |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Pick one thing. Not twelve. |
| 2 | A few small actions, chosen for the time you actually have. |
| 3 | On hard days, shrink the plan instead of skipping it. |
| 4 | Your real record — nothing invented. |
| 5 | A hard week makes next week lighter, never heavier. |
| 6 | Thirty days, in your own words and real numbers. |

## Alternates

Tested phrasings that fit the voice, if a headline needs replacing:

```
Small actions. Real consistency.
Keep going on difficult days.
Reduce the requirement, not the commitment.
The plan adapts to you.
Today is the only screen that matters.
```

## Rules

Never use:

```
CRUSH YOUR GOALS
BECOME UNSTOPPABLE
Don't break your streak!
Transform your life in 30 days
The #1 habit app
```

Specifically avoid:

- **Guaranteed outcomes.** "Build discipline in 30 days" promises a result. "One
  meaningful goal. Thirty days." states what the product is.
- **Streak or loss language.** It contradicts Minimum Day, which exists precisely
  so a bad day is not a failure.
- **Superlatives and rankings.** Unverifiable, and the guidelines prohibit them.
- **Health, medical or financial claims.** The app is not any of those things.
- **Exclamation marks.** One is too many for this voice.

## Localisation

English only for 1.0. If the listing is localised later, translate the meaning
rather than the words — "Progress without perfection" is an idiom and will not
survive a literal translation.
